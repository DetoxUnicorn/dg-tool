Vehicle Placarding Load Score (NZS 5433 Aggregate Multipliers)

Under NZS 5433, placarding mandates are calculated across mixed loads using risk multipliers against a 500-factor limit:

Current Load Aggregate Factor: 0 / 500 Factor Points
🟢
UNDER PLACARDING LIMIT: Placards not legally mandated under NZS 5433 (Load score < 500 points).
Class 1 Explosives / 5.2 Peroxides: Factor $\times 10$ (Threshold 50 kg)
Class 2.1 / 2.3 Gases & Class 3 PG I/II: Factor $\times 2$ (Threshold 250 units)
Class 8 / Class 9 / All PG III Goods: Factor $\times 1$ (Threshold 500 units)
Limited Quantities (LQ): Excluded from general placard score up to 1,000 kg total gross mass.
Lyttelton & Homer Tunnel Bylaws
🛑
Lyttelton Port Tunnel Bylaw: Bulk Class 2.1 Flammable Gas tankers (UN 1075 LPG) and Class 1 Explosives are strictly prohibited during normal daytime traffic. Vehicles must travel via Evans Pass or utilize scheduled nighttime escort transits.
⚠️
Homer Tunnel (Milford Sound Highway): Bulk placarded fuel tankers and dangerous goods loads exceeding placard thresholds require pre-notified transit escort by Waka Kotahi NZTA contractors.
Cook Strait Ferries (Maritime NZ Part 24B)
Freight-Only Sailing Mandate (Cat D & E): High-bulk flammable fuels, bulk LPG, and toxic compressed gases cannot sail with fare-paying passengers. Must book dedicated freight sailings.
Accommodation Clearance: Minimum 12m horizontal buffer from passenger observation decks, cabins, and life-saving gear.
Reefer Clearance: Minimum 3m physical clearance from 415V ferry shore-power cables and running diesel units.
