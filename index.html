Cook Strait Ferries (Bluebridge / Interislander)

Inter-island Ro-Ro transport is governed strictly by Maritime New Zealand Part 24B and vessel safety management plans.

⚠️
Weather Deck Only Directives: Class 2.1 Flammable Gases (LPG UN 1075) and high-volatility Class 3 fuels must be booked for open weather deck stowage only. Strictly prohibited in closed lower vehicle decks.
🛑
Reefer & Power Lines: Flammable gases and liquids must maintain minimum 3-metre clearance from operating mechanical refrigeration units and 415V ferry shore-power lines.
🐑
Livestock & Accommodation Buffer: Units placarded with Class 6.1 (Toxic), 2.3 (Toxic Gas), or 8 (Corrosive) cannot be stowed adjacent to live livestock transporters or passenger access ladders.

Emergency Action Code (Hazchem) Reference
1st Character (Number): Firefighting medium (1=Coarse spray, 2=Fine spray, 3=Foam, 4=Dry agent).
2nd Character (Letter): Risk of violent reaction, breathing apparatus requirement, and spill approach (Dilute vs. Contain).
Letter 'E': Public safety evacuation must be considered immediately.
