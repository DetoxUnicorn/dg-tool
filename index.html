Vehicle Placarding Thresholds (NZS 5433)

Class 1 (Explosives): 50 kg Gross
Requires front/rear hazard placards & approved segregation receptacle.
Class 2.1 / 2.3 Gases & Class 3 PG I / II: 250 Litres / Kg
Placarding required if total aggregate exceeds 250 units.
General Dangerous Goods (PG III, Class 8, Class 9): 500 Litres / Kg
Placards mandated on front & rear of vehicle or sides of container.
Limited Quantities (LQ) Total Load: 1,000 Kg
Only requires black & white square-on-point LQ markings when exceeding 8 tonnes total vehicle mass.

Foodstuff Loading Directives

Dangerous Goods must never be carried in the same compartment with food, beverages, animal feed, or food packaging unless separated by an approved airtight barrier or locked segregation device:

Absolute Prohibitions:
• Class 6.1 (Toxic) Substances & Class 2.3 (Toxic Gases)
• Class 8 (Corrosive Liquids) stored higher than or adjacent to food packaging.
• Class 5.1/5.2 oxidizers that may contaminate consumables.
