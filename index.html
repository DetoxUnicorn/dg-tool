Cook Strait Ferry Guidelines (Passenger vs. Dedicated Freight)

👥 Passenger Sailings (Daytime Standard)

🚫
IMDG Stowage Cat D & E: Strictly prohibited on passenger sailings. High bulk flammable fuels, toxic compressed gases, and unstable peroxides must be moved to freight-only sailings.
📐
12-Metre Accommodation Buffer: Class 1 explosives, toxic gases, and toxic chemicals must maintain a 12m horizontal buffer from passenger observation decks, cabins, and lifeboats.
🌬️
Weather-Deck Mandatory: Cat B and C substances (e.g. Bulk LPG UN 1075) can only sail on passenger ferries if placed in open weather-deck slots.

🚛 Dedicated Freight-Only Sailings

✅
Full IMDG Allocation: Authorised to carry Stowage Categories D & E. Accommodates standard Class 2.1 bulk road tankers and high-volume hazardous freight.
🛑
Reefer & Shore Power Clearance: 3-metre physical separation still mandated from running mechanical refrigeration diesel units and 415V electrical cords.
🐑
Livestock Buffer: Minimum longitudinal bay clearance required from live livestock trucks and driver accommodation quarters.

Hazchem Emergency Action Code Reference
1st Character (Number): Firefighting agent (1=Coarse spray, 2=Fine spray, 3=Foam, 4=Dry agent).
2nd Character (Letter): Violent reaction risk, breathing apparatus mandate, and spill response (Dilute vs. Contain).
Letter 'E': Public safety evacuation consideration is critical and urgent.
Driver Vehicle Emergency Kit Checklist

✅ Chemical-resistant overalls & rubber boots
